Lead progress – City Oasis

How many new enquiries Light Life and Secret Soak Society get each month, where they come from, and how many turn into hot leads. June – November 2026.

Source: GoHighLevel (contacts and lead pipelines), data pulled 18 Nov 2026. November 2026 is a part month (to 18 Nov). Lead records start in June 2026. Imported mailing lists are not counted as new leads. GHL does not record sales, sale values or response times, so these are not shown.

Summary

  • Leads fell by a third after July. About 120 new leads a month in June–July, about 84 a month in August–October. The low point was September (40). October recovered to 70.
  • Secret Soak Society now brings most of the leads. Its Facebook leads held steady at about 48 a month. Light Life's Facebook leads dropped from about 72 a month to 10–12 since September, after its ad budget was reduced.
  • The biggest leak is follow-up. Since 1 October, 102 of 121 new leads have not been marked hot, warm or cold, so nobody has qualified them yet. Before October almost every lead was followed up.
  • When leads are followed up, many are hot. In June–August, 41% of leads showed booking intent. That fell to 13% in October, mostly because fewer leads were followed up, not because lead quality changed.
  • Website forms bring very few leads. 18 since August across both brands, and 16 of those have never been followed up.
  • Imported lists are slow to qualify. About 17,000 old contacts were imported. 637 WhatsApp numbers were collected from them, and the marketing team reports 78 replies and 11 hot leads (these replies are not yet recorded in GHL).
  • Some people message us directly. 13 enquiries since September came in through Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp messages. None has been followed up, and the brand they asked about isn't recorded.

Status

Hot-lead rate
The share of all leads that the team marked hot (showed buying or booking intent), June to November. Example: 35% means about 1 in 3 leads was hot. Leads that were never followed up count as not hot.
Not followed up
Leads since 1 October that have not been marked hot, warm or cold, so nobody has qualified them yet. Example: 60 of 78 means 60 of the 78 leads since October are still waiting. Lower is better.
